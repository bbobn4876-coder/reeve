Streamer traffic,

Reeve Agency is a specialized influencer marketing and media buying agency focused on the iGaming industry. We operate at the intersection of online casinos, betting, and streaming platforms, organizing effective integrations with top-tier and rising streamers on Twitch and Kick. Our team handles the full cycle of influencer collaboration: from selecting relevant channels and negotiating formats (giveaways, branding, exclusive promo codes, interactive shows) to running social media promotions and driving audience retention.

Our core advantage is deep analytics and a data-driven approach. We don't just buy airtime — we optimize the conversion funnel, track FTDs (first-time deposits) and player LTV, and provide transparent reporting on every integration. With our own database of vetted streamers, VIP player management expertise, and a dedicated technical support team, we turn broadcasts into a stable stream of profitable traffic.

We know how to navigate strict platform moderation, build effective audience engagement on Kick and Twitch, and convert viewer interest into predictable, global growth for your brand.
